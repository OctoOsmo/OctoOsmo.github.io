finance-tracker

A personal, single-user tool that reads bank statement emails from its owner's own Gmail account and builds a private spending dashboard.

Privacy policy

  • The app requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) and is used only by its owner.
  • It reads only messages from the owner's banks and downloads their statement attachments.
  • All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
